Question Title: Advanced File Management

Question Link: https://my.newtonschool.co/playground/code/4ks5o139cfoh?lecture_hash=v210chxjexei

Solution:
mkdir project-files
cd project-files
touch report.docx summary.docx draft.docx
cp report.docx report_backup.docx
mkdir old
mv draft.docx old/
mv summary.docx final_summary.docx
cp final_summary.docx old/
rm report_backup.docx

Approach:
Created a folder called project-files.

Inside it, created three files:

report.docx

summary.docx

draft.docx

Made a copy of report.docx and name it report_backup.docx.

Created a new folder called old. Move draft.docx into old/.

Rename summary.docx to final_summary.docx.

Copy final_summary.docx into the old/ folder as well.

Delete report_backup.docx.
